Common Pitfalls

The 4 Traps That Can Cancel Your Medicaid

Even seniors who have received Medicaid for years can have their coverage abruptly cancelled if any of these four administrative triggers occur.

Asset Creep Above $1,600

Seniors retain $75/mo personal allowance. If unspent funds, tax refunds, or small birthday checks accumulate to $1,605 on the first of the month, DSS flags the case as asset-ineligible.

Even a $5 overage triggers denial

Undelivered State Mail

DSS sends Form W-1ER by regular postal mail. If the senior moved into a nursing facility or in with an adult child without updating their DSS mailing record, the renewal packet is returned to sender.

Automatic closure for non-response

Missing Verification Proofs

Submitting the renewal form without full bank statements, current gross pension stubs, or health insurance premium bills triggers a quick W-1348 with an uncompromising 10-day deadline.

Incomplete verification closures

Unreconciled COLA Increases

Annual Social Security Cost-of-Living Adjustments (COLA) change monthly gross income. If patient liability (applied income) is not accurately adjusted, nursing home billing conflicts arise.

Applied income billing errors

Emergency Grace Period

Has Your Medicaid Coverage Already Been Terminated?

If you received a Notice of Discontinuance from Connecticut DSS, do not panic. You have statutory reinstatement rights under state and federal law.

The 90-Day Reconsideration Window

Under Connecticut DSS rules, if an individual’s Medicaid is closed for failure to complete an annual renewal, they have a 90-day grace period from the effective date of closure to submit the missing renewal form and verification proofs.

When submitted properly within this 90-day window, DSS is required to process the renewal without requiring a brand-new application and must restore Medicaid coverage retroactively to the date of closure, erasing any private-pay debt accrued during the lapse.

Compass specializes in emergency 90-day reinstatements to restore dropped benefits immediately.
