Our Genesis & Purpose

Why Compass Was Created

Every month in Connecticut, hundreds of seniors and their adult children face devastating private-pay nursing home rates exceeding $16,000, sudden discharge notices, or confusing 10-day DSS verification deadlines. Compass was founded to replace panic with precision.

The Bureaucratic Breakdown

Connecticut DSS caseworkers shoulder intense caseloads, often managing over 400 complex files simultaneously. Under state statutes, caseworkers cannot act as your personal researcher, accountant, or family advocate. If a document is missing or formatted ambiguously, the system generates a standard Form W-1348 with an uncompromising 10-day deadline.

When families cannot obtain 60 months of bank records, life insurance surrender valuations, or property deeds within that window, applications are automatically denied—leaving families facing retroactive private-pay liability.

Result: Unnecessary Denials & Family Financial Depletion

The Compass Solution

Compass serves as the professional bridge. Because our leadership worked directly inside the Connecticut DSS eligibility apparatus, we understand precisely how caseworkers evaluate submissions, what forensic documentation satisfies strict Uniform Policy Manual (UPM) rules, and how to anticipate questions before they become denial notices.

We conduct comprehensive 5-year lookback audits before filing, package every transaction with clear paper trails, manage all communication with the state, and protect your loved one’s dignity throughout the entire continuum of care.

Result: Meticulous Compliance & Total Family Peace of Mind

The Compass Methodology

5 Operational Pillars That Ensure Success

Every Connecticut Medicaid file we accept is guided by five systematic pillars designed to eliminate errors, streamline verification, and accelerate approval.

01

Forensic Pre-Audit

We perform an exhaustive 60-month audit of all checking, savings, brokerage, annuity, and property records before DSS sees them. We identify uncompensated transfers, unexplained cash withdrawals, and titled assets in advance, crafting compliant documentation that prevents penalty periods.

02

Anticipatory Indexing

Caseworkers appreciate structured files. We package your application with indexed cover sheets, itemized reconciliation tables, and policy citations that align directly with the Connecticut DSS Uniform Policy Manual (UPM), making it easy for the caseworker to check every statutory box.

03

W-1348 Emergency Protocol

When DSS issues Form W-1348 (Proof We Need), the clock starts ticking on an unforgiving 10-day window. Compass acts instantly—tracking down missing statements, drafting formal extension requests if financial institutions delay, and uploading verified records via the ConneCT portal.

04

Continuum Coordination

Medicaid is not just paperwork; it is about care. We coordinate directly with nursing home business office managers, hospital discharge social workers, and home health coordinators to confirm "Medicaid Pending" status and prevent aggressive billing or premature discharge notices.

05

Annual Redetermination Shield

Medicaid approval is not permanent; Connecticut requires rigorous annual redeterminations. We manage ongoing renewals, track asset thresholds ($1,600 for individual HUSKY C recipients), and verify that coverage remains uninterrupted year after year.
