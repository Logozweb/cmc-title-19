Dignified Independence

Why Move to a Nursing Home When You Can Receive Care at Home?

Over 90% of Connecticut seniors express a strong desire to age in place within the familiar surroundings of their own homes and communities. However, as medical conditions worsen, family caregivers become overwhelmed by the daily physical demands of bathing, medication management, and mobility.

Paying privately for 30 to 40 hours per week of home health aides quickly runs between $4,000 and $6,000 per month out of pocket—an impossible expense for most retired Connecticut families.

The Connecticut Home Care Program for Elders (CHCPE) was created by the state to bridge this exact divide. It provides state- and Medicaid-funded care packages that keep seniors safely in their homes at a fraction of the cost of institutional placement.

Program Architecture

Understanding the 3 Tiers of the Connecticut Home Care Program

The CHCPE is divided into three distinct categories based on your loved one’s level of physical need and financial assets.

Tier 1 State-Funded

Category 1: Moderate Need

Designed for seniors who are at risk of hospitalization but do not yet meet the strict nursing facility level of care.

  • Clinical Need: Assistance with 1 to 2 critical ADLs.
  • Asset Limits: Up to $45,864 (single) / $61,152 (married).
  • Services: Limited homemaker, companion, and meal delivery.
Subject to state budget waitlists.
Tier 2 State-Funded

Category 2: Intermediate Need

For seniors who clinically meet nursing home level of care but hold financial assets above standard Medicaid limits.

  • Clinical Need: Meets nursing home level of care (3+ ADLs).
  • Asset Limits: Up to $45,864 (single) / $61,152 (married).
  • Cost Share: Mandatory 4.5% or 7% copay based on monthly income.
Allows higher assets while receiving robust care.
Highest Coverage Level
Tier 3 1915(c) Medicaid Waiver

Category 3: Full Medicaid Waiver

The comprehensive Medicaid waiver tier providing extensive daily aide hours, zero copayments, and complete state funding.

  • Clinical Need: Full nursing facility level of care.
  • Asset Limit: $1,600 (single) + Spousal Impoverishment Protections.
  • 5-Year Lookback: Strict 60-month DSS financial audit applies.
  • Cost Share: 100% funded with zero mandatory monthly copays.
Compass specializes in Category 3 approvals.

Services Covered Under the Connecticut Waiver

When approved for CHCPE, an individualized Plan of Care (POC) is developed to wrap critical services around your loved one.

Personal Care Assistants (PCAs)

Hands-on assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, toilet assistance, transferring, and personal hygiene.

Homemaker & Companion Care

Meal preparation, light housekeeping, laundry, grocery shopping, prescription pickup, and cognitive stimulation.

Adult Day Health Centers

Daytime structured social activities, nurse health monitoring, hot meals, and transportation to licensed adult day centers.

Emergency Response Systems

24/7 personal emergency response pendants, wristbands, and automatic fall detection systems installed in the home.

Home-Delivered Meals

Nutritionally balanced hot or frozen meals delivered directly to the senior’s door through approved regional vendors.

Environmental Accessibility

Minor home modifications including bathroom grab bars, wheelchair exterior ramps, handrails, and safety adaptations.

Dual-Track Bureaucracy

The Dual Assessment Challenge in Connecticut

Unlike standard Medicaid, applying for home care waivers in Connecticut requires navigating two completely separate state agencies at the exact same time.

The Two Parallel Approval Tracks

Both must be satisfied simultaneously for services to begin

If either track stalls, the entire home care plan is delayed. Families frequently pass the in-home nurse assessment with flying colors, only to have their application denied 60 days later by a DSS financial caseworker over an unanswered Form W-1348 verification request.

Track 1: Clinical Level of Care

Conducted by your regional Access Agency (such as Connecticut Community Care, Southwestern CT Agency on Aging, or Agency on Aging of South Central CT) and Maximus/Ascend.

Evaluates cognitive safety, physical ADL score, and in-home care plan viability.

Track 2: DSS Financial Eligibility

Conducted directly by the Connecticut Department of Social Services (DSS) eligibility operations units.

Audits 60 months of banking, assets, spend-down compliance, and issues Form W-1348.
